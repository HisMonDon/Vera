Vera — Free AP & IB Physics Video Lessons

Vera is a free, curriculum-aligned online physics platform built for AP and IB Physics students. It breaks each course into short, structured video lessons with clear visuals, guided examples, and walkthroughs of real past exam questions, with saved progress once you create an account.

Courses

  • IB Physics HL — Complete International Baccalaureate Higher Level physics curriculum with focus on experimental skills and data analysis.
  • IB Physics SL — Core International Baccalaureate Standard Level physics curriculum, building a strong foundation in key physics principles.
  • AP Physics 1 — Preparation videos for the AP Physics 1 exam covering kinematics, Newton's laws, circular motion, and simple harmonic oscillators.
  • AP Physics 2 — Algebra-based physics covering fluid mechanics, thermodynamics, electricity, magnetism, optics, and quantum phenomena.
  • Grade 11 Physics — Videos and tutorials for the Grade 11 Physics Ontario curriculum.
  • Grade 12 Physics — Videos and tutorials for the Grade 12 Physics Ontario curriculum.

Topics Covered

Introduction to Physics, Kinematics, Forces and Dynamics, Work and Energy, Circuits and Electricity, Momentum and Collisions, Magnetism, Optics, Light, Fluids, Harmonics, Electrostatics, Rotational Motion, Thermal Physics, and Modern Physics.

About

Vera is built and maintained by Chenyu Lu, founder of his school's Physics Club, along with student contributors who help create tutorials. Source code is available on GitHub.
